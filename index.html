Gonzaga Basketball

The Greatest Mid-major of all time

Gonzaga basketball has become one of the most successful college basketball programs in the country. Based in Spokane, Washington, the Bulldogs are known for their consistent success, strong player development, and passionate fan base. Gonzaga has made a name for itself nationally through deep NCAA Tournament runs, talented players, and a culture built around teamwork and competing at a high level.

Gonzaga basketball is deeply connected to the Spokane community and has built a reputation that extends far beyond the university. Over the years, Gonzaga has developed into a national basketball powerhouse while maintaining the close-knit culture that makes the program unique.

Three facts about Earth

  • Gonzaga has made the NCAA Tournament every year since 1999, one of the longest active streaks in college basketball.
  • The Bulldogs have reached the NCAA Championship game twice, in 2017 and 2021.
  • More than 20 Gonzaga players have gone on to play in the NBA, including stars such as John Stockton, Adam Morrison, Domantas Sabonis, and Rui Hachimura.
